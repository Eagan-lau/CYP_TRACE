# AI assistance disclosure

The author reported using ChatGPT 5.6. Assistance covered study planning,
analysis/visualization code, manuscript drafting and revision, and reference
checking. Numerical estimates were produced by executed code on the cited
datasets; the language model did not supply CYP evidence labels. The authors
reviewed and edited generated material and remain responsible for the work.
The model designation follows the author's disclosure rather than a reconstructed
provider-side record of every session.

Representative author instructions, translated from Chinese and previously
included in the manuscript, were:

- “Starting from the raw data, repeat the calculations for the entire paper.”
- “Keep the existing story and revise in the order: correct substantive
  errors, strengthen comparisons, clarify the strategy, and simplify expression.”
- “Check whether the citations and reference contents are accurate.”

These examples document the nature of assistance, not an executable research
protocol. Implemented calculations are specified in the source code, methods
and frozen inputs. Verification distinguishes file integrity, saved-prediction
recalculation, biological data reconstruction and model inference. Reported
test coverage is documented in REPRODUCTION_GUIDE.md and the retained test
results. A passed subset test is not described as a complete model refit.
