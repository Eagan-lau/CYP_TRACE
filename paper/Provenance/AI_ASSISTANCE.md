# AI assistance

OpenAI ChatGPT (model version 5.6, as reported by the author) assisted study
planning, analysis and visualization code, manuscript drafting and revision,
reference checking and schematic layout. The authors reviewed and edited the
generated material and take responsibility for the work.

Representative author prompts, translated from Chinese:

- "Starting from the raw data, repeat the calculations for the entire paper."
- "Keep the existing story and revise in the order: correct substantive errors,
  strengthen comparisons, clarify the strategy, and simplify expression."
- "Check whether the citations and reference contents are accurate."

Biological labels originate from the cited resources. Numerical results are
computed by the supplied analysis code. Reproduction commands, input hashes
and test scope are documented in `paper/REPRODUCTION_GUIDE.md`.
